              <p class="pub-desc lang-en">One goal-driven visual data system. Qwen-Image, SAM3, Hunyuan3D 2.1, and Blender form the asset pipeline. A VLM/CV quality gate diagnoses viewpoint failures and selects among 24 repair actions before LoRA adaptation.</p>

              <div id="work-workbench" class="project-update">
                <span id="dataevolver-v2"></span>
                <h4 data-i18n-en="DataEvolver Workbench" data-i18n-zh="DataEvolver 工作台">DataEvolver Workbench</h4>
                <p class="pub-desc lang-en">The Workbench brings model configuration, task execution, and data review into one interface, with independent worker review. Status reads use snapshots; execution writes pass through the state store.</p>
                <p class="pub-desc lang-zh">工作台集中展示模型配置、任务执行与数据复核，并加入独立工作单元复核。状态查询读取快照，执行写入通过状态存储校验。</p>
                <p class="pub-desc lang-en">A GenEval retrospective audit covers 6,636 images. Under 415 qualified historical references, field-projection probes identify 42 of 45 known scoring defects, with 1 false alarm among 370 non-defect references. GPU end-to-end validation remains pending.</p>
                <p class="pub-desc lang-zh">GenEval 回顾审计覆盖 6,636 张图像。在 415 个合格历史参考条件下，字段投影探针命中 45 处已知评分缺陷中的 42 处，在 370 个非缺陷参考中误报 1 处。GPU 端到端验证仍待完成。</p>

                Introduces a two-stage geometry-texture decoupled <strong>Preset LoRA</strong> paradigm on Qwen-Image-Edit-2511: Stage 1 (Rank=128) learns explicit 3D spatial rotation on white studio backgrounds; Stage 2 (Rank=64) freezes the base to specialize in natural scene boundary &amp; lighting fusion (+50% training efficiency). Combined with 6-stage <strong>Additive Rehearsal</strong> across 30°–180°, 11 specialized models are deployed in production on <strong>TeleStudio</strong> across 3 clusters (16× H100 GPUs, +15% editing accuracy &amp; aesthetics).
              </p>
              <p class="pub-desc lang-zh">
                提出二阶段几何与纹理分离训练范式（Preset LoRA）：一阶段利用高容量 LoRA（Rank=128）在白底环境显式学习三维旋转几何变换；二阶段冻结基座并引入轻量残差 LoRA（Rank=64）专精复杂背景边缘融合与光影协调（整体训练效率提升 50%）；设计基于角度拓扑距离衰减的 6 阶段增量数据重放（Additive Rehearsal），11 个分角度专用模型在 TeleStudio 平台规模化上线（3 集群 16 卡 H100，编辑精度与美学指标提升 15%+）。
              </p>
